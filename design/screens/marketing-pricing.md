## Plan cards

| Plan | Price | Brands | Prompts | Cadence | Seats | Rechecks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $99/mo | 2 | 20 | Monthly | 1 | 2/mo |
| Agency | $249/mo | 10 | 20 | Weekly | 3 | 10/mo |
| Studio | $799/mo | 25 | 30 | Weekly | 10 | 100/mo |

Card bullets must match plan gates:

- Starter: monthly, lighter Home, CiteBrief sender, 1 seat, PDF + client link, basic actions, no command center, no extra brands
- Agency: weekly Friday, white-label, client CC, history, 3 seats, Slack, command center (scorecards, competitors, opportunities, risks, pipeline), extra brands $29
- Studio: everything in Agency + custom sender/domain, 30 prompts, 10 seats, bulk send, portfolio CSV; ChatGPT / Gemini / Grok / AI Overviews. Mark Later studio-only bullets as (coming) when shown: client portal archive, priority support, internal COGS export

No free forever. Trial: 14 days, 1 brand, 5 prompts, ChatGPT + Gemini only, 1 full run. Claude paused on all plans. Annual = 10 months prepaid.

FAQ: outgrow Starter → Agency; client CC is Agency+ only; extra brands Agency+ only; extra runs $9 after monthly re-check credits on paid plans.
